  • Crates: back in the alleyway beside the Gelateria. They were part of the fish market model, so they had slid out with the stall.
  • Fish: each fish remembers a fixed resting spot, which was still the old one. All 12 now point inside the moved tank, and they sit exactly on those spots. The fish caught in the drying nets had the same problem and are fixed too.
