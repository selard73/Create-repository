Squirrel card layouts

Three ways to fit the squirrel card above the hotbar. Each is drawn at half size in your Studio window (1672 by 445) and on a phone in landscape (844 by 390). The grey tiles are the Roblox tool icons; the card never crosses them (the previews are fixed-size now, so they no longer squash on a narrow browser). Every option gets the carousel arrows.

A Two columns

Portrait on the left; the name and bio sit together as one block, centred in the right column, with the bio at a fixed readable size. Uses the width that short screens have plenty of; the portrait stays big and spinnable.

C Two columns with a face strip

Option A plus a row of small faces along the bottom of the text column: the neighbours in the album order, the current one ringed, greyed if not found yet. Tap a face to jump, or use the arrows. A little busier, but you can see where you are in the album.
