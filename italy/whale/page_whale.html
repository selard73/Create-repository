How he will behave

He swims a closed loop out at sea: from the mouth of the bay out and across to the cave and back. Cruising, his back and the little dorsal fin ride just above the water, so from the promenade and the beach you see a blue shape cutting the surface and the tail moving under it.

About every three minutes (wherever you put a blow station on the route) he slows, stops, rises a touch, and blows: a white spout of mist about a third of his length high, with a sound. Then the classic dive: nose down, flukes lifting clear of the water, and he slides under. He travels submerged for half a minute, then rises and cruises on.

Every player sees the same whale at the same spot at the same time, because his position is worked out from the server clock, not from each phone. Nothing is heavy: one mesh, one script, one particle emitter.

Your picks

  • Size. I suggest 60 studs nose to flukes (about 12 player-heights, 20 studs tall). Smaller (45) reads as "a whale somewhere out there"; bigger (80) is a real giant and needs deep water. Easy to change later with one line.
  • Where exactly. I need to measure the sea first (a read-only survey: water level, how deep it is, where the cave and the bay mouth are, what is in the way). Then I draw the route on a picture for you before he is placed.
  • Speed and pauses. 7 studs a second, 12-second pauses to blow. If the trip ends up longer or shorter than three minutes each way, I add or move blow stations.
  • The blow sound. Pick one from the Toolbox the way you picked the waterfall ("whale blow" or "whale spout" search well) and send me the id, or say "you pick".
  • Who watches. Later the Sunbather and the shore squirrels can react ("Did you SEE that?") through their speech bubbles. Not in this first pass.
Honest limits. If the sea near the bay is only 10 or 12 studs deep, a 60-stud whale cannot sink fully out of sight on his dive; he will dip and the flukes will still lift, but his belly stays near the bed. The survey tells us; a deeper route or a smaller whale both fix it.
