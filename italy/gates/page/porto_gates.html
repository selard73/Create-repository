What the play tests showed

  1. 7 Harbour finds: both harbour gates shut, every wall solid, the notes read "Find 10 squirrels in The Harbour to open this gate / 7 / 10 found so far". Walking into the Porta del Borgo stops you at the doors. Standing in the town sends you straight back to the gate with France's message.
  2. 10 Harbour finds: the doors and the iron gate swing open with the creak, the walls let you through, and you can walk up the town stairs and stay there.
  3. 10 Harbour + 7 Via della Piazza: the five Groves gates stay shut with their notes. Standing in the meadow, on the pebble beach or on the lemon terraces sends you back to the nearest gate on the town side.
  4. 10 Via della Piazza: all five swing open (the beach rope is put away), you can walk into the meadow and down the Beach steps.
  5. The map: Via della Piazza and The Groves are covered until their gates open for you, and each Porto area shows its own count.
  6. No wall stands on a path anywhere along either line, checked stud by stud. The Flower Meadow gate moved 9 studs up the path so the watchtower path is all behind it.
