## The remake pipeline (works - copy it)
Scripts in **roblox-props/tools/remake/** (copied from the old chat's scratchpad) and roblox-props/squirrels/.
1. `python tools/remake/unzip_any.py <id> "<zip glob>" src_vN` - moves current files to <id>/old_oct5/ (MOVE, no extra disk), unzips with plain names (`Meshy_AI_new_texture.*`; Meshy names can contain curly quotes).
2. `python tools/remake/prep_any.py <id> src_vN` - premerge 280k -> 15k tris, prep_squirrel. Look at ref_front.png + ref_side.png (side view: z runs right->left, feet at px 645, 202 px/unit; front: x=(px-400)/204).
3. `RIGARGS="<tail_xmax> <body_x> 99 99 <head_ymax> <head_zmin> [tail_xmin]" COLORBOOST=1.12 CONTRAST=1.05 python tools/remake/rig_any.py rig <id>` - writes the sharpened 1k colour texture (UnsharpMask x2, saturation/contrast boost; she wants crisp, NOT faded - captain used 1.18/1.08), gray, rig + audit. body_x = head centre x from the front view.
4. `REGIONS='[{"box":[x0,x1,y0,y1,z0,z1],"from":["Head","Neck"],"to":"Chest"}]' TAILYMIN=0.5 python tools/remake/rig_any.py fixw <id> <neck z from audit>` - also renders turn_q34.png + turn_close.png and probes feet. ALWAYS look at turn_close.png: stretched cheeks/fins/paws = a box too wide or too narrow. squirrels/probe_headw.py <dir> <id> x0 x1 z0 z1 maps head weights to separate an arm from a cheek. Props held in front -> Chest; a salute touching the hat -> keep the arm ON THE HEAD (Chest/Root -> Head box) - pinning it smeared the paw. Tail curling near the head -> Tail1/Tail2 -> Head box (Signora Chiave).
5. Swap: tools/italy_squirrels/swap_model.template.lua (fill %ID% %OLDFX% %OLDFY% %OLDSX% %NEWFX% %NEWFY% %OLDFLIP%; rename "_old_oct5'" to a new suffix), `python tools/italy_squirrels/pack.py swap_X.lua sX.rbxmx SwapX`.
