# Treehouse mailbox - the first 10 letters (draft for Shannon)

One letter a day (the daily card's day, 09:00 UTC), only from a squirrel the player has found - except the
welcome letter, which arrives the moment the mailbox appears (the first treehouse piece). Letters never pile up:
only one waits at a time, and the next is written the day after the last one was read. Each letter opens
with "Dear <player's name>," and ends with the sign-off and the squirrel's name. Acorns are tucked in where
it says so.

1. **Forest Ranger Squirrel** - always, the welcome - 15 acorns
   > Welcome to your new treehouse! As Forest Ranger I checked the ladder twice and the rope three times. Rule
   > number one: always climb facing the ladder. Rule number two: check this mailbox every day. The squirrels LOVE
   > writing letters.
   > P.S. I tucked a few acorns in the envelope. Every new home needs a little something.
   > *Your neighbour on the trail,*

2. **Baking Betty Squirrel** - 10 acorns
   > I baked you a welcome-home cupcake with extra frosting and a little acorn on top. Then I ate it. Quality
   > control, you understand. I will bake you another one tomorrow. Probably.
   > P.S. Here are some acorns instead. They are crunchier.
   > *Sprinkles and hugs,*

3. **Mr. Holmes Squirrel**
   > I have been watching your treehouse for three days. Purely for investigative reasons. My conclusion: somebody
   > lives there, and that somebody is you. Case closed. I will send my bill in the morning. (There is no bill. I just
   > like saying that.)
   > *Discreetly yours,*

4. **Gordo Squirrel** - 10 acorns
   > Do you have any snacks in your treehouse? I am asking for a friend. The friend is me. If you ever find an acorn
   > under your rug, it is mine. Please keep it safe until I come over for a nap.
   > P.S. I put some acorns in here for you. It was very, very hard.
   > *Your hungry friend,*

5. **Fairy Squirrel** - 20 acorns
   > It is Tuesday somewhere, so I am granting you one small wish. I wished you a really good acorn, and then a few
   > more because I sneezed. Sorry about the glitter on the envelope. And on the mailbox. And on the tree.
   > *Twinkles,*

6. **Buccaneer Squirrel**
   > Ahoy, <name>! I found a treasure map that leads straight to your treehouse. I followed it all morning and ended
   > up at the bakery. Twice. If you spot a big X anywhere near your ladder, send word at once. The map may have been
   > upside down.
   > *Arr and ahoy,*

7. **El Scientifico**
   > I measured your treehouse with my Acornometer. Results: one hundred percent cozy, zero percent explosions. Very
   > disappointing for science, very good for you. I have attached my findings. They are this letter.
   > *For science,*

8. **Surfer Dude Squirrel**
   > Duuude! Your treehouse is SO high up. If it rains enough, do you think the whole forest turns into one giant
   > wave? Asking for science. And surfing. Mostly surfing. If it happens, I call the first ride.
   > *Hang ten, dude,*

9. **Bird Watching Squirrel**
   > A bird landed on your roof this morning. I wrote it down. It was a brown one. Page five of my bird list is now
   > all about your treehouse. I hope you do not mind. Please wave next time. The bird will not, but I will.
   > *Binoculars up,*

10. **Rainy Day Squirrel** - 10 acorns
   > The clouds over your treehouse look suspicious. I have sent you a spare umbrella in this envelope. It is very
   > small because it had to fit. Also my spare-spare umbrella. Also some acorns, in case the umbrellas do not work.
   > *Stay dry,*

Gifts total 65 acorns over the ten letters. Change any words, gifts or the order (before they go live - after that,
new letters are only ever added at the end).
