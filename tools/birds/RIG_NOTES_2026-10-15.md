## Bones (Blender export, the same export settings as the whale, crab and octopus)

Pelican: Root (no deform) > Body > Chest > Neck1 > Neck2 > Head > Jaw; Body > Tail; Chest > Wing.L > WingTip.L (and .R);
Body > Thigh.L > Shin.L > Foot.L (and .R). The gull is the same with one Neck and no Jaw.
Positions per bone (Blender and Roblox model space, studs at the exported size) are in `pelican_bones.json` / `gull_bones.json`.
Import brings them in small (the FBX carries cm units, as the whale did: 1.9 studs, then ScaleTo); ScaleTo keeps the bones.
Expect the importer's InitialPoses folder + AnimationController (harmless) and possibly a rotated PivotOffset on the MeshPart
(the octopus needed it set to identity).

## Moving the bones from a script

Roblox model space after import: the bird faces -Z, up is +Y, ITS LEFT is -X (Blender x is mirrored). Rotate each bone about a
MODEL axis at its own joint: at rest, `axisLocal = bone.WorldCFrame:VectorToObjectSpace(model:GetPivot():VectorToWorldSpace(A))`,
then `bone.Transform = CFrame.fromAxisAngle(axisLocal, angle)`. Tested ranges (pictures):

| motion | bone | model axis A | angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| wing up (flap) | Wing.L / Wing.R | +Z | L: -0..-55 deg, R: +0..+55 deg |
| wing tip follow-through | WingTip.L / .R | +Z | L: -0..-15, R: +0..+15 |
| wing down beat | Wing.L / .R | +Z | L: up to +10, R: up to -10 |
| head turn to its left / right | Head | +Y | +35 / -35 |
| head nod down | Head (or Neck) | +X | negative |
| pelican beak open / pouch gulp | Jaw | +X | 0..-20 (more stretches the throat) |
| leg back (step, or tucked) | Thigh.L / .R | +X | negative swings back, positive forward (waddle used about 25) |
| body roll (waddle) | Body | +Z | +-7 |

## What these two models can and cannot do

The wings are folded shells painted onto the body (one surface where they touch it). Lifted up to about 55 degrees they read as a
cartoon flap. Spread further, for a flying pose, they show rough hidden mesh under the wing. So they suit PERCHED birds:
the pelican on a post or the quay and gulls on railings and roofs that look around, flap, hop, waddle and (pelican) gulp. The
circling flock in BirdsClient would need either the part-built birds kept for flight or new Meshy models generated in a
wings-spread flying pose, which can be rigged the same way. BirdsClient v2 flaps only parts named LeftWing/RightWing, so a
bone-driven template needs a small client change.
