## What makes the references look realistic (runner 7's read of the pictures)

**Garden (ref_01..03)**
- A slow river with a soft surface and wet rocks along its edges; mossy boulders.
- A bright red arched (taiko-style) bridge; a waterfall over mossy rock in the background.
- Japanese maples in autumn orange/red, with layered leaf cards (not solid leaf balls); a twisted black pine.
- Tall, dense grass along everything (knee-high blades), purple hydrangea clumps.
- A grey cobble path with large flat stepping slabs set into it, round stones; a raked-sand (white gravel) bed.
- Stone lanterns (toro) with glowing paper windows; small low path lights along the walk.
- A torii gate behind the bridge.
- Light: soft, slightly overcast, gentle haze.

**Town street (ref_04..09)**
- Two-storey wooden shopfronts (machiya) with open fronts: wooden counters, rope-bound wooden tubs with little paper
  price tags, slatted produce crates (greens, beans, red peppers), a chalk A-frame sign out front.
- Strings of round cream paper lanterns with a red crest, hung on wires across the street.
- White-plaster-and-dark-timber houses with grey tile roofs and lattice windows; a larger temple hall with red
  railings and gold bracket details; a big red torii over the street; stone stairs with metal rails.
- Grey stone-slab paving with real texture and a kerb; trees growing out of planters; bollards; a neon sign far off.
- (Their streets are big and empty; ours should be fuller.)
