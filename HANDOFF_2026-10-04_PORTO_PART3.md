## Gotchas learned today
- computer-use `type`: 17+ chars goes through HER clipboard (16 is fine). Keep chunks <=15.
- Python heredocs turned `\\n` into REAL newlines inside Lua strings twice -> build escapes with chr(92) or a .py file, and run the odd-quote check (make_client_fix.py does).
- Studio log cuts a warn at ~700 chars: dump sources in 450-char chunks; multi-line text continues on following log lines (parse until the next timestamp).
- Stopping a play test hangs Studio ~30 s (90k-part teardown); wait for "teardown completed" in the log; sometimes the first Stop click doesn't register - screenshot before editing (edits during a test are lost).
- In a Device-Simulator play test the device list is locked; switch device in edit mode.
- MeshPart TextureID alpha shows the part's colour (not see-through): use SurfaceAppearance AlphaMode=Transparency.
- Model:GetBoundingBox overlap probes give false positives on sloped things; check real geometry.
- Auto-mode classifier sometimes blocks the first installer import; Shannon re-confirms ("you may run ...").
- Phones: no overlaps ever; the crab panel places itself clear of all drawn UI + Roblox's hotbar; the Daily card may overlap (Shannon: it is closed right away).
- Stop at 70% weekly usage (57% at handoff).
